How to use this kit

  1. Enter your current available cash in Overview.
  2. Add invoices and update them when customers pay.
  3. Enter expected income and spending in the 13-week forecast.
  4. Record actual money received and spent.
  5. Use the follow-up messages to remind customers about unpaid invoices.
  6. Export a full backup regularly to protect your records.
This tool helps organise your records and estimates. It does not connect to your bank, verify payments or guarantee future income.
